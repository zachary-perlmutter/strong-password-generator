Privacy Policy — Strong Password Generator

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Strong Password Generator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

All passwords are generated entirely within your browser. Nothing you generate is sent to any server, logged, or shared with any third party, including the developer.

Permissions used

clipboardWrite — used only to copy a generated password to your clipboard when you click the copy button.

storage — used only to remember your Advanced panel preferences (password length and character options) locally via Chrome's sync storage, tied to your own Google account. This data is never accessible to the developer.

Third parties

This extension contains no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts.
